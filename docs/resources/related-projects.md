# Related projects

Gold Pasal does not replace the other projects under `~/dev`. Use these links for a specific job; do not maintain duplicate notes here.

## Personal engineering notes

- `~/dev/docs` is the canonical prose garden for durable personal notes and recaps.
- `~/dev/brain` renders those notes and owns the personal desk/garden experience.

## External learning atlas
